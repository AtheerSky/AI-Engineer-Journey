The data

Three independent sources. One decision-ready dataset.

Open-Meteo · Weather API

Daily weather

Radiation, temperature, humidity, pressure, wind, cloud cover, sunshine duration and precipitation — one row per city per day.

Open-Meteo · Air Quality API

Hourly air quality

PM10, PM2.5, CO, NO₂, O₃, SO₂ and US AQI. Hourly by nature, so we averaged each day to match the weather's grain.

Project dataset

Solar generation

Daily output per configuration, with panel type, mount type, tilt and efficiency across five Saudi cities.

Why unify them?
  • Weather explains environmental conditions.
  • Air quality explains atmospheric conditions.
  • Solar output provides the operational outcome.
  • Together they allow the model to learn the complete system.

10,980 daily records · 5 cities · 366 days of 2024 · 6 panel-and-mount configurations per city-day.

The agent layer

Advice that has to cite something.

Retrieve → rank → cite

Scientific evidence

The dust-risk prediction picks the query itself: dust-focused when risk is elevated, weather-focused otherwise. Papers come from arXiv, are ranked by TF-IDF cosine similarity, and the closest one is cited next to the recommendation — with a bundled library of real published papers as the offline fallback.

Parse → predict → ground

Ask SolarIQ

Ask in plain English. It reads the city, panel type, mount type and whether you mean today or tomorrow, fetches that day's conditions, predicts with Model 1A, and answers with its two closest knowledge-base facts — each shown with its similarity score, so the grounding is visible rather than claimed.
